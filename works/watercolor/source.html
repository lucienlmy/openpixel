// A sheet of cold-pressed paper on a desk, a tin of six pans and a water jar. Brush with your finger and the paper takes
// water and pigment; while it is wet the colour creeps outward, mixes where it meets another, runs to the wet edge and
// dries there darker, and settles into the grain of the paper. Tap into a wet wash to drop pure pigment and watch it
// bloom; hold still to drip clean water and push the colour back into a cauliflower edge. Pick a pan to change colour.
